MSMED Act s.2(b) & s.15 · Income-tax Act s.43B(h)

Ledgers know when you were invoiced.
Only evidence knows when goods were accepted.

Under s.43B(h), a payment to a micro or small supplier is deductible this year only if it is made by the MSMED deadline, and that deadline runs from acceptance, not the invoice date. AcceptGuard reads delivery challans, emails, letters and contracts with Gemini, checks every quoted date against the source, computes the deadline in deterministic Python, and refuses to answer when the evidence does not support one.

    Who does what

    Gemini extracts

    One structured-output call per document returns each date exactly as written, its ISO value and the sentence it came from. The schema has no field for legal judgments or confidence scores.

    Tesseract verifies images

    Photos and scans are OCR'd independently. The exact date token must appear in the OCR text, or the date is not used.

    Python decides

    Quotes must exist word-for-word in the source, dates are re-parsed without guessing, and conflicts abstain. Acceptance and deadlines follow fixed MSMED rules.

    Accountant signs off

    Every output is a candidate for review. Contested interpretations are labelled, and uncertain cases are routed to a person instead of answered.
